# Meridian Bahamas Run

## Marina

Meridian Marina

## Project Topic

Events / Campaign

## Objective

Plan and promote a VIP Bahamas Run for existing Meridian Marina customers and guests with boat storage. The event is intended to create a premium customer experience, strengthen loyalty with Meridian customers, deepen the relationship between Meridian Marina and Bluff House, and introduce customers to Bluff House as a destination.

## Current Status

Planning

## Background / Context

Meridian Marina is planning a group boating trip for existing customers and guests who have boat storage at Meridian Marina. Participants will travel in their own personal boats from Florida to the Bahamas, with Bluff House serving as the primary destination.

The event is designed as a customer loyalty and relationship-building experience, not only as a promotional campaign. The target goal is approximately 8-12 boat sign-ups.

## Dates

- Primary trip dates: August 13-18
- Backup trip dates: August 20-25
- Trip length: 5 days
- Registration deadline: July 15 or until spaces are filled
- Availability: First come, first served
