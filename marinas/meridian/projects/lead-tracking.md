# Meridian Lead Tracking Cleanup

**Marina:** Meridian
**Project ID:** MER-LEAD-001
**Project Topic:** Lead Tracking

---

## Objective

Clean up the lead tracking process at Meridian. Separate lead source from contact method. Ensure all inbound leads are attributed to the correct marketing channel, not the contact pathway.

---

## Current Status

In Progress — Field definitions being finalized. Staff process update and CRM/form update pending.

---

## Background / Context

Currently, "Website" is being used as a lead source when it should be a contact method. A lead that comes from Google Ads and submits a form should be attributed to Google Ads, not the website. The lead tracking sheet needs two separate fields: one for the source (where the lead came from) and one for the contact method (how they reached out). Staff need to be trained on the distinction.
