## Objective

Support the Meridian Bahamas Run as the destination property by coordinating rooms, slips, special rates, welcome party details, onsite guest experience, and destination logistics.

---

## Role of Bluff House

Bluff House is the primary destination for the Meridian Bahamas Run. Meridian owns customer sign-ups and primary customer communication. Bluff House supports the destination experience.

---

## Key Responsibilities

Confirm rooms and room availability. Confirm slips and dockage logistics. Confirm special rates. Support welcome party planning. Provide destination details for promotional materials. Provide information for participant checklist and FAQ. Coordinate onsite guest experience. Support final itinerary once the participant group is confirmed.

---

## Dependencies

Confirmed participant count from Meridian. Confirmed attendee sheet. Room/slip availability. Special rates. Route and arrival plan. Welcome party details. Final itinerary.

---

## Tracking Requirements

Coordinate with MER-EVT-001 registration process. Ensure room/slip reservations are tied back to confirmed participants. Use UTMs, QR codes, or vanity URLs if Bluff House-specific promotional materials are used. Track destination readiness and event attendance where possible.
