# Portfolio-Wide Conversion Tracking Setup

**Project ID:** CORP-TRK-001 | **Scope:** Corporate / All Marinas | **Status:** In Progress | **Priority:** High

---

## Objective

Create consistent conversion tracking standards across all VIP Marinas properties so marketing can measure form fills, phone calls, text leads, booking actions, campaign performance, and lead quality.

---

## Systems Involved

- GA4
- Google Ads
- Website forms
- Phone/call tracking
- Kenect/text leads
- Booking engines
- CRM or lead sheets
- UTMs
- Reporting dashboards

---

## Tracking Areas

- Form submissions, phone calls, click-to-call, email clicks
- Booking clicks and completions
- Kenect/text leads
- Event registrations
- Lead source and contact method
- Campaign attribution
- Offline conversion outcomes
