# Portfolio Brand and Messaging Standards

**Project ID:** CORP-BRAND-001 | **Scope:** Corporate / All Marinas | **Status:** Planning | **Priority:** Medium

---

## Objective

Create brand and messaging standards that help VIP Marinas maintain consistency across properties while allowing each marina to keep its local personality.

---

## Key Areas

Portfolio-wide brand voice, marina-specific messaging, campaign messaging standards, visual direction, color usage, photography/video standards, print vs. digital creative, event branding, and customer experience language.

---

## Current Notes

For Meridian Marina and Bahamas Run creative, use the Meridian-style color scheme: blues/navy with aqua, turquoise, seafoam accents, and clean nautical white space. Print can rely more heavily on the color palette. Digital may need more photography but should still incorporate the Meridian colors where possible.
