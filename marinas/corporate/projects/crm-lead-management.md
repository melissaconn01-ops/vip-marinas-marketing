# Portfolio-Wide CRM and Lead Management Setup

**Project ID:** CORP-CRM-001 | **Scope:** Corporate / All Marinas | **Status:** Planning | **Priority:** High

---

## Objective

Create a consistent lead management structure across all VIP Marinas properties, including lead source, contact method, lifecycle stages, owner/follow-up process, and lead-to-booking reporting.

---

## Key Areas

- Lead source definitions
- Contact method definitions
- Lead type definitions
- Marina/location field
- Campaign field
- Lifecycle stage
- Follow-up status
- Lost reason
- Booking/revenue outcome

---

## Important Rule

Website is not a lead source. Website is a contact path or conversion location. The true source should come from UTMs, referrer data, campaign data, call tracking, self-reported source, or CRM/source fields.

---

## Dependencies

CRM decision, lead sheet review, marina team adoption, dev/form support, vendor support.

---

## Leadership Approval Needed

Yes

4. Define handoff and follow-up process.
